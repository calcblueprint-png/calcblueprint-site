This Privacy Policy describes how CalcBlueprint ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit calcblueprint.com.

We automatically collect non-personal information:

We use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and advertising. Third parties we work with include:

We use the information collected to operate and improve the Site, analyze usage, display relevant advertisements, and respond to inquiries.
