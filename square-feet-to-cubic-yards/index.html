Square Feet to Cubic Yards

By Emiliano · Last updated 2026-11-13

Square feet is an area; cubic yards is a volume. To convert one to the other, you need a third dimension — depth or thickness. Once you have that, the formula is square feet × depth in inches ÷ 324 = cubic yards. The table below handles the most common depths; the calculator at the top does the arithmetic for you.

Why 324? One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet (3 × 3 × 3). One foot is 12 inches. So 27 × 12 = 324. Dividing square feet times inches by 324 gives you cubic yards directly.

The Formula

The conversion from square feet to cubic yards is a two-step calculation that combines area, depth, and volume:

Cubic yards = (Square feet × Depth in inches) ÷ 324

If you want to do it in cubic feet first (because that's how most people think about volume), the formula is:

Cubic feet = Square feet × (Depth in inches ÷ 12)
Cubic yards = Cubic feet ÷ 27

The two formulas produce the same answer. The 324 shortcut just combines them into one step.

Worked Examples

Three real-world examples showing how this conversion is used in practice:

Example 1: Concrete slab for a shed

A 12 × 16 foot shed base is 192 square feet. At 4 inches thick, cubic yards = (192 × 4) ÷ 324 = 2.37 cubic yards. Add 5–10% for waste and you order 2.5 to 2.6 cubic yards of ready-mix concrete.

Example 2: Gravel driveway

A 20 × 40 foot driveway is 800 square feet. At 6 inches deep, cubic yards = (800 × 6) ÷ 324 = 14.81 cubic yards. Gravel is sold by the cubic yard or by the ton, so you'd order 15 cubic yards or about 21 tons (at 1.4 tons per cubic yard).

Example 3: Mulch for a garden bed

A 300 square foot bed at 3 inches deep needs (300 × 3) ÷ 324 = 2.78 cubic yards. Bagged mulch is sold in 2 cubic foot bags; 2.78 cubic yards = 75 cubic feet = about 38 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert square feet to cubic yards?

Multiply square footage by depth in inches, then divide by 324. Formula: cubic yards = (square feet × depth in inches) ÷ 324. The 324 comes from 27 cubic feet per cubic yard times 12 inches per foot. You need the depth — square feet alone cannot become cubic yards without a third dimension.

How many cubic yards is 1,000 square feet?

It depends on depth. 1,000 square feet at 1 inch deep is 3.09 cubic yards. At 4 inches, it is 12.35 cubic yards. At 6 inches, 18.52 cubic yards. At 12 inches, 37.04 cubic yards. Multiply your square footage by depth in inches, then divide by 324.

How many cubic yards of concrete for a 10x10 slab?

A 10 by 10 foot slab (100 square feet) at 4 inches thick needs 1.23 cubic yards of concrete. At 5 inches, 1.54 cubic yards. At 6 inches, 1.85 cubic yards. Use the formula (100 × thickness in inches) ÷ 324. Order 5 to 10 percent extra for spillage.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

There are 27 cubic feet in one cubic yard (3 feet × 3 feet × 3 feet = 27 cubic feet). To convert cubic feet to cubic yards, divide by 27. To convert square feet to cubic yards, you must first multiply by depth in feet to get cubic feet, then divide by 27 — which is the same as square feet times depth in inches divided by 324.
