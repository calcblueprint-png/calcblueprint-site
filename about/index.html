About CalcBlueprint

CalcBlueprint builds free construction calculators for contractors, estimators, and serious DIYers.

Why We Built This

Most construction calculators online are buried behind ads, require email signup, or give vague ranges instead of real numbers. We built CalcBlueprint to be different: enter your measurements, get your materials and costs. No friction.

How Our Calculators Work

Every calculator uses industry-standard formulas from ACI, NRCA, and manufacturer specifications. Inputs are simple — dimensions, material types, and unit prices. Outputs are specific: cubic yards, bag counts, linear feet, and total project cost.

What Our Results Mean

Our results are estimates for planning, not engineering specifications. Always verify critical calculations with a licensed contractor or engineer before ordering materials or starting work. Local codes, site conditions, and material variations can affect actual requirements.

Independence

CalcBlueprint is an independent resource. We may earn a commission if you purchase materials through partner links, at no cost to you. This never affects the accuracy of our calculators.
